# /conclave-epic

Keep the epic layer alive after `/conclave-init`. Epics are created at inception; this command adds, revises, decomposes, or drops them as the product learns — typically right after a [`/conclave-close`](/en/commands/close) review proposed it.

```
/conclave-epic new
/conclave-epic edit EP-NNN
/conclave-epic split EP-NNN
/conclave-epic retire EP-NNN
```

**Stories are not written here.** An epic carries rough `## Candidate stories`; `/conclave-planning` refines them when the epic's roadmap slot comes up. Nothing is committed.

Requires a v2 workspace (`conclave_version >= 2.0.0` and `product/epics/`) and a clean working tree.

## Sub-actions at a glance

| Sub-action | Subagent | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `new` | Product Manager, then Scrum Master | Writes `EP-NNN-<slug>.md` with `status: proposed` and slots it into future roadmap slots. |
| `edit EP-NNN` | Product Manager (+ SM if size/priority changed) | Revises the epic; keeps ID, status, stories, roadmap slots. |
| `split EP-NNN` | Product Manager, then Scrum Master | Decomposes into 2–4 child epics; parent becomes `retired`. |
| `retire EP-NNN` | **None** | Mechanical: epic and its unstarted stories → `retired`, roadmap cleaned. |

## `new`

Asks for a title, the outcome it should deliver, type (`feature | enabler`) and priority (`must | should | could`, default `should`). The PM writes the epic against `vision.md`, checking existing epics to avoid overlap. The Scrum Master then places it into future `planned` slots by priority and dependencies — never touching `active` or `closed` slots. You confirm the slot change before `roadmap.md` (with a re-plan log row) and the epic's `roadmap_slots` are written.

## `edit EP-NNN`

Refuses `retired` and `done` epics (*"create a new epic instead"*). Asks what should change; the PM returns the full epic, preserving ID, `status`, `stories:`, `roadmap_slots` and `created_at`. If `size` or `priority` changed, the SM re-slots it.

## `split EP-NNN`

Refuses `retired` and `done` epics. Asks how many children (2, 3 or 4) and the split axis. Hard rule: every non-retired, not-done story of the parent and every candidate story lands in exactly one child; otherwise the PM returns `SPLIT_UNSAFE: <reason>` and nothing is written. The orchestrator re-checks count and coverage mechanically.

On success: children get `split_from: EP-NNN` and `status: proposed` (or `active` if any of their stories is in the active sprint); moved stories get their `epic:` updated, as does the backlog's Epic column; the parent becomes `retired` with `superseded_by` and keeps its `done` stories. The SM re-slots the children.

## `retire EP-NNN`

No subagent. Refuses when any of the epic's stories is `in-progress`, `review` or `verified`, and refuses `done` epics. Asks why (non-empty). Then:

- Epic → `status: retired`, `retirement_reason`, `retired_at`.
- Its `backlog` / `ready` stories → `retired` (same fields as `/conclave-story retire`).
- Removed from every `planned` roadmap slot; a slot left empty is marked `planned (empty)` and you are told to fill it. A re-plan log row is added.

## Epic state machine

```
proposed ──/conclave-planning──→ active ──/conclave-close──→ done
    ↘                               ↘
      retired  (/conclave-epic retire | split)
```

See the [state machine](/en/state-machine#epics) page for details.

## Guardrails

- Never commits, pushes, or opens a PR.
- Writes only under `conclave/product/` (epics, roadmap, backlog) and the story frontmatter fields `epic`, `status` and retirement fields.
- Never writes story files.
- Never modifies `active` or `closed` roadmap slots.
