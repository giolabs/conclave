# Team profiles

Conclave separates **structural invariants** (you cannot do Scrum without them) from **optional gates** the team chooses to commit to. The split is controlled by the `team_profile` field in `conclave/config.md`.

## Always required (structural — never skippable)

These are enforced by every team profile:

- **Sprint Planning** — without a goal and a locked story list, there is no sprint. Enforced by `/conclave-planning`.
- **QA verification of acceptance criteria** — every `done` story carries a verification report appended to its acceptance file. Enforced by `/conclave-qa`.
- **Sprint Review** — runs inside `/conclave-close`. Without inspecting the Increment the sprint never closes, velocity is never recorded, and the next sprint cannot be planned.
- **Acceptance criteria on every story** — Gherkin scenarios in `acceptance/AC-US-NNN.md`. Stories without them fail the DoR.
- **A `discipline` assigned to every story** (`frontend | backend | qa | design | devops | mobile | multi`) — assigned by the Tech Lead during `/conclave-planning`, checked by the DoR.
- **Definition of Done compliance** — the team-customized DoD checklist must be met for every story.

## Optional per profile

| Gate | Where it runs | Config key | `lean` | `full-scrum` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tech Lead PR approval | `/conclave-pr-review` | `ceremonies.peer_pr_review.required` | off | on |
| Sprint Retrospective | inside `/conclave-close` | `ceremonies.close.retro` | off | on |

There are no standup, grooming, review or retro commands. The [board](/en/commands/board) and story statuses are the daily view, refinement happens inline in `/conclave-planning`, and review + retro are parts of `/conclave-close`.

> **Removed in v2.0.0:** `ceremonies.daily_standup`, `ceremonies.backlog_grooming`, `ceremonies.sprint_review`, `ceremonies.sprint_retrospective`. A config that still has them gets one warning and they are ignored. `/conclave-init --upgrade` removes them and maps `sprint_retrospective` to `close.retro`.

## Profile semantics

- **`lean`** — structural invariants only. For solo devs, very small teams (2–3), and internal tooling work.
- **`full-scrum`** — TL PR approval gate and retrospective on. For cross-functional teams that ship to external stakeholders.
- **`custom`** — the team sets `ceremonies.peer_pr_review.required` and `ceremonies.close.retro` individually. Recorded as `custom` so nobody assumes a preset.

## Gates that cannot be turned off

`sprint_planning` and `qa_verification` cannot be flagged off — setting `required: false` for either is rejected. Sprint Review has no flag at all: `/conclave-close` always runs it.

## The PR-approval gate

When `peer_pr_review.required: true`, the **Tech Lead** (or designated approver) runs `gh pr review --approve` via `/conclave-pr-review US-NNN`. QA's job ends at the verification report and a PR comment — never an approval. See the [state machine](/en/state-machine) for the transitions.

When the flag is `false`, QA's pass moves the story straight from `review` to `done`.

## `team_mode` is separate from `team_profile`

`team_mode` (`solo | team`, set once by `/conclave-init`) controls the **roster shape**. `team_profile` controls **which optional gates are on**. Solo projects are always `lean`; a team can pick any of the three profiles.

## Changing your profile

Edit `team_profile` in `conclave/config.md`. To override a single gate without changing the whole profile, set `team_profile: custom` and edit `ceremonies.peer_pr_review.required` or `ceremonies.close.retro`.

Changes take effect on the next slash-command run — flags are read at invocation time.
